# Investment Analyst

A Streamlit-based investment research dashboard that turns a list of stock tickers into a structured analyst-style report.

The application is designed to make daily stock research quick and consistent. It combines market data, valuation, business fundamentals, growth, risk, analyst consensus, recent news, news summaries, catalysts, earnings information and upcoming key dates into one report.

> **Important:** This application is a research and analysis tool. It is not financial advice and it does not guarantee investment returns. Always verify important information independently before making an investment decision.

---

## 1. What the App Does

The Investment Analyst analyses the stocks selected by the user and produces two levels of information:

### Summary

The summary provides a quick portfolio-level view, including:

- BUY / HOLD / SELL action for each stock
- A short justification for each action
- Overall score for each stock
- Best opportunity
- Highest-risk stock
- Most attractive valuation
- Overall recent-news sentiment
- Strongest business
- Strongest catalyst
- Immediate upcoming key dates
- Number of companies analysed
- Positive, neutral and negative news article counts

### Detailed Stock Report

Each stock receives a detailed report covering:

- Company name
- Ticker
- Sector
- Overall score
- Model verdict
- BUY / HOLD / SELL action
- Reason for the action
- Business quality
- Growth
- Valuation
- Forward P/E
- Trailing P/E
- PEG ratio
- Analyst consensus
- Analyst count
- Average analyst price target
- Current price
- Target upside
- High analyst target
- Low analyst target
- Risk assessment
- Beta
- Catalyst strength
- Recent news
- News sentiment
- News summaries
- Overall news sentiment
- Upcoming key dates
